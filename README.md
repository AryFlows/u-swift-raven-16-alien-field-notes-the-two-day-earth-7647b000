An alien tourist's one-line review of Earth, written to `/output/entry.txt` by `main.ts` (Node, stdlib only).

## The entry (148 characters)

> Earth: the locals scoop their pets' droppings into little bags and carry them home like treasure. Bizarre, touching — five stars, would visit again.

## Run

```sh
node main.ts
```

`MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` sets the output directory (defaults to `/output`).

## Gate

`entry.txt` is UTF-8 text of 1–200 characters, a single non-blank line. `main.ts` enforces the 1–200 character and single-line guards before writing.

## Why this line

One joke, landed cleanly at the end: the alien watches humans bag their pets' droppings and carry them home like treasure. It reads as a real tourist review — a verdict and a five-star rating in the closing words — and the laugh comes from the visitor's outside view of ordinary Earth life, not from a joke bolted onto the premise.
